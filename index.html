Anatomy of a stop

A stop is a sequence. We teach it in order.

Almost everything that determines how a stop goes is decided in the first few moments. The program walks through those moments one at a time, so the right response is already familiar when it matters.

0:00

Lights behind you

Signal, slow down, and choose a lit, open place to pull over. Where you stop is your first decision.

0:20

Stopped and still

Engine off, window down, dome light on after dark. Hands stay on the wheel where they can be seen.

0:45

First contact

The officer speaks first. Answer plainly, keep your voice level, and say what you're about to do before you do it.

1:30

Reaching for documents

Name where your license and registration are, then move slowly. No sudden reaches, no surprises.

3:00

If the tone shifts

Stay still and keep your voice even. You can decline a search without arguing — the roadside is not where a case is won.

5:00+

Ending the stop

Ask whether you're free to go. Note the agency and badge number. Anything you dispute, you dispute on paper, later.

Training modules

Four modules. One skill each.

Each module is a short video with a facilitator prompt, built to run inside a single class period. The full library is password-protected for enrolled partners.

01

The First Five Minutes

Pulling over, positioning, hands, and the opening exchange.

 02

Communication Skills

Tone, word choice, and answering questions without escalating.

 03

De-Escalation Lite

Reading tension early and lowering it with posture and pace.

 04

Consent and Final Steps

Searches, what consent means, and how to close the stop.
