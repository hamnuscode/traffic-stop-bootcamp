Four modules · Watch in order

The program, module by module.

Watch straight through the first time, in order — each module builds on the one before it. Then work back through the moments that matter most for your group.

Module 01

The Ideal Traffic Stop

This module puts you in the shoes of a police officer as they approach a driver during a traffic stop. The driver improves his trust with the police officer by taking five important actions. We cover the importance of stopping in a well-lighted place and the need to put on your hazard lights as you pull over. During this time, you are keeping your hands on the steering wheel and making sure the interior lights enable the police to see in your rear compartment.

Module 02

Communication Skills

In this module, you will learn three elements of communication with police officers. We discuss the typical questions and topics that will surface during the traffic stop. You will learn how to re-direct personal questions that have nothing to do with the traffic stop, and when to remain silent.

Module 02 Watch in order
Module 03

Diffusing a High-Tension Situation

In this segment of the training you will learn about triggers and behaviors that cause a traffic stop to end in a confrontation. We then look at the concept of “contempt of cop”. Many of these behaviors challenge the authority of the police officer. We examine examples of this in the context of a traffic stop. Trainees learn how to diffuse the situation with a three-step process.

Module 03 Watch in order
Module 04

Replying to a Request to Search & Ending the Traffic Stop

This module gives the driver guidelines on what to do if the police ask to search your car. We recommend that you do not agree to let the officer search your car unless they have a warrant that is signed by a judge. We then describe the five reasons why officers can search your car without your permission. Special focus is put on what to say if you are arrested. Finally, we look at how to determine if you are free to go and how to end the traffic stop.
